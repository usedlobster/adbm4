# Privacy Policy

We believe in a simple, private web. This site does not use third-party trackers, behavioral advertising, or external analytics scripts.

### User Account Information

To provide the booking service, we store the name and email address provided during the registration process.

- **Purpose:** This data is used exclusively to manage site access, identify users for specific bookings, and send system alerts (e.g., booking approvals).
- **Retention:** We retain this information for the duration of the project or until we are notified that a user is no longer authorized by their employer.
- **Access:** Users may request to see or correct their stored information at any time via their company administrator.



### What we store on your device

- **Session Cookie:** A standard PHP session cookie (PHPSESSID) is used to keep you logged in. It is strictly necessary for the site to function and expires when you close your browser.
- **Local Storage:** We use your browser's local storage to remember interface preferences (such as dark/light mode) so you don't have to reset them manually.
- **Consent Choice:** We store a small flag to remember if you have dismissed our informational banner.

### What we log on our server

When you access this site, our server logs your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This data is used exclusively for:

- **Debugging:** Fixing errors and improving site performance.
- **Security:** Identifying and blocking malicious activity or unauthorized access attempts.
- **Basic Stats:** Counting total page views for internal capacity reporting.

We do not share, sell, or distribute this data to third parties unless required to do so by law.


### External Links

Our site may contain links to external services (such as Google Maps for site directions). Clicking these links takes you to their platform, where their own privacy policies and tracking practices apply.
