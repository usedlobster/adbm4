
Privacy Policy

We believe in a simple, private web. This site does not use third-party trackers, behavioral advertising, or external analytics scripts.

1. What we store on your device

Session Cookie:

A standard PHP session cookie (PHPSESSID) is used to keep you logged in.
It is strictly necessary for the site to function and expires when you close your browser.

Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to remember your interface preferences (such as dark/light mode) so you don't have to reset them every time you visit.

Consent Choice: We store a small flag to remember if you've dismissed our informational banner.

2. What we log on our server

When you access this site, our server logs your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit.
We use this data exclusively for:
Debugging: Fixing errors and improving site performance.
Security: Identifying and blocking malicious bot activity.
Basic Stats: Counting total page views for internal reporting.

We do not share this data with third parties.

3. External Links

External Links: Our site contains links to external services like Google Maps.
Clicking these links will take you to their platform, where their own privacy policies and tracking practices apply.
